## Context

What situation makes a decision necessary? What forces (technical, product, organizational) are in tension? Link to the repository state, prior ADRs, or documents that motivate this one.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and unambiguously. This is the part every other document quotes or points back to.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Include real trade-offs, not just benefits — an ADR that lists no cost is probably missing something.

## Alternatives Considered

What else was on the table, and why it was not chosen. An alternative rejected without a stated reason invites the same debate to repeat later.

## Follow-up Actions

Concrete next steps this decision implies, and which future sprint/milestone owns each one. An ADR that changes nothing about what happens next is a note, not a decision.
